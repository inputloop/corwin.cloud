The web server at corwin.cloud keeps a log of each request: the address that asked, the time, the page (without anything after a question mark), whether the page was found, and the browser's description of itself (kept to guess which requests are abuse). It keeps that log for up to 14 days, in a file of this site's own, to keep the site working and to guess which requests are abuse. When a request goes wrong, it also notes in an error log the address that asked, the time, the page, anything after a question mark in its address, how it asked (such as GET), the protocol version, the page that linked here (if your browser said), and the site's name; it keeps that for up to 14 days, in a file of this site's own, to keep the site working. Both are on the server, not in your browser, and forgetting here does not touch it.
